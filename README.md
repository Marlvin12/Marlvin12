## Currently Working On

### [LOKO](https://www.lokoglobal.org/data) — Physical AI Infrastructure

I'm the co-founder of LOKO, where we're building infrastructure for collecting real-world, egocentric data to support robotics and embodied AI research.

Our work focuses on connecting AI research organizations with diverse physical environments, capturing human demonstrations, and developing the operational infrastructure needed for reliable data collection.

### Research — Trustworthy AI & EEG Security

My current research investigates **backdoor attacks and data poisoning in EEG-based deep learning models** used in brain-computer interfaces.

I'm exploring how Euclidean alignment, adversarial training, and spectral analysis can help improve the security and robustness of neural decoding systems.
