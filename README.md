## About Me

I grew up in **Gweru, Zimbabwe**, and moved to the United States on a full scholarship to study computer science.

I am interested in the point where software leaves the screen and begins coordinating the real world: transportation, commerce, payments, communication, and other systems that people depend on every day.

Today, I am building **[Loko](https://www.lokoglobal.org/)**—an AI-powered infrastructure layer designed to help people discover, coordinate, and pay for services across fragmented and informal markets.

My work sits at the intersection of:

* AI agents and conversational interfaces
* Real-time and distributed systems
* Mobility, logistics, and marketplace infrastructure
* Applied machine learning
* Technology for emerging markets

## What I Am Building

### Loko

**Loko is an AI agent for the real world.**

Instead of navigating several disconnected applications, a user can ask Loko to coordinate services such as transportation, food, deliveries, stays, errands, and local commerce.

The broader technical challenge is not simply building another application. It is creating the infrastructure through which AI agents can reliably interact with merchants, drivers, payment systems, and customers operating across fragmented markets.

Current areas of work include:

* Conversational commerce through messaging platforms
* Real-time mobility and delivery coordination
* Merchant onboarding and service discovery
* Multi-sided marketplace infrastructure
* Payment, trust, and risk systems
* AI-assisted operations and customer support

## Research Direction

The questions I care about are larger than any single application:

1. How can AI agents reliably perform real-world tasks when data, payments, and services are fragmented?

2. How should agents coordinate among customers, merchants, drivers, and institutions when each participant has different incentives?

3. How can intelligent systems establish trust in markets that still depend heavily on cash, messaging applications, and informal relationships?

4. How can advanced AI become useful to the next billion users without requiring them to change how they already communicate and transact?

5. How can machine-learning systems remain accurate, explainable, and computationally affordable in resource-constrained environments?
